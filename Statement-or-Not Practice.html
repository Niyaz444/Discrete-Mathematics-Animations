Reference — is it a statement?

  • A statement is a declarative sentence with exactly one truth value — True or False, never both, never neither.
  • Questions, commands, and exclamations are never statements — nothing to check.
  • Sentences with a free variable (like x + 5 = 12) are open sentences, not statements, until the variable is fixed.
  • Opinions are not statements — no shared, objective test decides them.
  • A self-referential sentence that flips its own truth value (a paradox) is not a statement.
  • A statement only needs a truth value in principle — "currently unknown to us" is still a statement, just one we can't yet score.
