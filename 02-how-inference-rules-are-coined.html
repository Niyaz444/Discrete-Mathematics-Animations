Take everything you're given (the premises), AND them together, then ask: does that combination → imply the conclusion, no matter what truth values p, q, r… take? If the final column is True in every single row — a tautology — the rule is valid, forever, for any statements you plug in. If even one row comes out False, it isn't a rule at all; it's a fallacy.
