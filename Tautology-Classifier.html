Reference — the three classifications

  • Tautology — True in every row of the truth table, no matter the inputs.
  • Contradiction (fallacy) — False in every row, no matter the inputs.
  • Contingency — True in some rows, False in others; genuinely depends on the inputs.
  • A formula is satisfiable if it's True in at least one row — every tautology and every contingency qualifies. Only contradictions are unsatisfiable.
